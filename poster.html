ORBITGUARD
PRE-COOL

Landing-warning control for lunar radiator dust protection

Mohimanti Misson · Mechanical Engineering · Eastern Michigan University

THE PROBLEM

Lunar landing dust can compromise exposed thermal radiators. Closing a shield reduces dust exposure but can also reduce heat rejection. How can a lunar robot manage both risks?

THE IDEA

Use a predicted landing warning to open the radiator in advance, remove heat, and then protect the radiator with a moving cover as the dust event begins.

THE APP

Mobile mission replay, interactive thermal scenarios, baseline comparison, CSV export, camera color sampling, and a physical prototype measurement lab.

METHOD

Single-node radiative energy balance:

C·dT/dt = Qin − εσA(T⁴−Tspace⁴)

Example inputs: C=1800 J/K; A=0.06 m²; emissivity open/covered 0.80/0.18; 10-second integration step.

EXPERIMENT

Run a reactive cover and Pre-Cool controller with identical starting temperature, heat input, and dust timing. Compare cover-open time during the dusty interval and peak temperature.

LIMITATIONS

Simulation only. Cover-open time is an exposure proxy, not measured dust mass. Parameters are illustrative; tabletop tests in air cannot validate vacuum thermal performance.

LIVE DEMO
