What the public data measures

  1. Cover movement. A NASA-funded team reported opening and closing in under two seconds during lab tests. Use two seconds as an illustrative budget, then measure your own flap. Louver test report
  2. Visible dust. Apollo 12 photographs showed dust 52 seconds before touchdown. Blue Ghost footage showed plume–surface interaction at about 15 meters altitude. Neither observation gives dust arrival time at a particular nearby robot. Apollo analysis · Blue Ghost video
  3. Temperature. LISTER provides separate lunar subsurface data. Your mock-up needs its own electronics-temperature measurements. LISTER archive

Build the tabletop scene

Materials: Two small cardboard boxes, foil, tape, a phone, and a dark backdrop. Label one box “Lunar robot” and the other “Lander.” Give the robot a front foil panel and a top-hinged cardboard flap. Mark “Dust reaches robot” with a colored paper card.

  1. Time the cover. Film the flap from the side. Time from its first movement until fully shut. Repeat three times and enter the slowest time above.
  2. Replay the landing. In the 60-second example, show the warning at 0 s, start moving the flap no later than 6 s, show the dust card at 8 s, and mark touchdown at 60 s. Use a stopwatch. This is an illustrative replay that treats the Apollo observation as a stand-in for dust at the robot.
  3. Measure cooling separately. Put a covered mug of warm tap water inside the robot box with a thermometer probe in the water. In the first run, leave the flap open before the marked dust event. In the second run, leave it closed during preparation. Give both runs the same starting temperature, water volume, and timing. Close the flap for the simulated dust event and reopen afterward in both runs. Record the temperature at the same points in each run.

The cardboard scene demonstrates motion, timing, and cooling in air. A longer tabletop run can make temperature differences easier to measure. Treat it as a separate test rather than stretching the historical landing timeline.
